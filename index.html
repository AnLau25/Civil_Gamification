How to play

Excavator Run

Tap anywhere to jumpSpace bar

The excavator runs by itself and speeds up as you go. Jump the barriers, the pipes and the cones. Clip one and a question stops you cold.

Right answer and you carry on with a bigger streak multiplier. Wrong answer ends the run. Three correct in a row earns a 50/50 you can spend on a question that has you stuck.

Gold Miner

Tap to drop the clawSpace bar

The claw swings over the shaft. Drop it at the right moment and it hauls back whatever it grabs. Heavy things come back slowly, so a boulder can eat half your shift while the diamond you wanted sits there.

Hit the money quota before the clock runs out. Between shifts you work the payroll: every question you get right grows the pot, one wrong answer wipes whatever you have not banked. Banked money buys dynamite, a strength drink and the rest.

Watershed Defense

Tap a unit, then tap the groundTap a unit to upgrade

Contaminants flow down the channel toward the river. Each treatment unit only works on what it actually treats, so a bar screen will not touch dissolved metal and UV will not settle grit. Read what is coming, then build for it.

Every unit has two upgrade paths of three tiers, and only one path can go past tier one, so each build is a real decision. Grant questions between waves pay for it.

How the questions work

  1. Set a name once. It follows you across all three games and puts you on the board.
  2. Filter by topic. Every game lets you drill Statics, Water and Wastewater, Geotech or whatever is hurting, on either exam track.
  3. Questions repeat on purpose. Anything you miss comes back sooner. Anything you answer correctly three times running fades out.
  4. Read the explanation. The short paragraph after each answer is where the marks actually come from.
